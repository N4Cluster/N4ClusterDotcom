Questions owners ask us

Text DEMO to (629) 290-1191 or email contacts@n4cluster.com. A founder answers.
Is it really commission-free?+

Yes. You pay $99 a month plus $0.50 per order, and standard card processing (2.9% + 30¢). On a $40 order, you keep $38.04.

What does my customer pay?+

Your menu prices, plus a flat $0.99 service fee at checkout. That fee is on top of the order, not taken out of your share.

Do I have to quit DoorDash or Uber Eats?+

No. Keep them for discovery. N4Cluster gives the guests who already know you a cheaper, direct way to order again.

I don't have a website. Is that a problem?+

Not at all. We'll help you set up a domain and build your ordering site for you.

What if I want to cancel?+

It's month-to-month. Your first 30 days are free, and you can export your customer data whenever you like.
